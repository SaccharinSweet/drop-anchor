Drop anchor, little tiger

For when a big feeling grabs the wheel. Even the fiercest small tiger gets caught in weather sometimes. Take a few minutes to hold your ground. The storm may not pass, and that’s okay. The point is to stop drifting with it.

This uses the ACE steps of “dropping anchor” from acceptance and commitment therapy, as taught by Dr. Russ Harris: acknowledge your thoughts and feelings, come back into your body, engage with what you’re doing. Loop through them slowly three or four times. It isn’t a relaxation trick; the feelings are allowed to stay.
